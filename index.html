About Me

Hi there! My name is Jackson Gwaltney. I am proud homeschooled student who lives in Chesapeake Virginia. Much of my coding experiance at first was gained from a genuine desire to learn. My first larger project was a creature game similar to pokemon that was text based and made with python. A personal project like this taught me the value of consistant effort and increased my joy for coding. I then learned Java and spent time prompting ChatGPT for real life mini projects that I could build to increase my logic and problem solving skills. As of now, I am aspiring to become a developer and to enter into the proffesional space while still continuing to learn on my own for the sake of increasing my opportunities. Although I am undoubtably excited to lean into the world of development, I will always try to keep an open mind so that I can live my life as an ongoing experiment which the LORD will guide me through.
