About Me

Hi, I’m Jackson, an aspiring web developer who enjoys turning ideas into clear, useful, and engaging websites. I’m currently building my skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I enjoy learning how thoughtful design and clean code can work together to create a great experience. I like taking a project from an initial idea to a finished page, figuring out how to organize the content, choose a visual style, and make everything work smoothly. Each project gives me a chance to practice new skills, solve problems, and improve my attention to detail. I’m excited to keep learning, take on new challenges, and grow as a developer. Feel free to explore my projects and see what I’ve been working on.
